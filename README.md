# Fennix AI Integration for n8n

Fennix AI is a Decision Intelligence platform that helps businesses connect scattered data, analyze business signals, and make smarter decisions using AI.

## Features

- Connect business data sources
- Analyze real-time business signals
- Generate AI-powered insights
- Support smarter decision-making workflows

## About Fennix AI

Fennix transforms business data into actionable intelligence by combining internal and external signals including market trends, operational data, and performance insights.
